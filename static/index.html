RENTAL AGREEMENT

THIS RENTAL AGREEMENT is made and executed on this 01ST Day of this April 2026, at Bangalore by and between:

Mr. MANOJ M & SANCHITHA C J, S/O T Mahesh, Aged 45, No,99,100 C Near Sri Kalikamba Temple, ChowdeshwariNagar, , Laggere, Bengaluru- 560 058, hereinafter called as the "OWNER", ONE PART,

AND

Mr. RAJUGOWDA S/O Subbegowda, Aged 47, No. 18, 3rd Cross, Rajeev Gandhi Nagar, Laggere, Bengaluru-560 058, hereinafter called as the TENANT, SECOND PART.

The TENANT has approached and requested the OWNER to rent out the building for Commercial Purpose No.99 & 100C, Near Sri Kalikamba Temple Chowdeshwari Nagar, Laggere, Bengaluru- 560 058, Consists One RCC Roofed Shops, with rolling Shutter and electricity, Toilet and water facility, the Tenant has approached the owner let out the schedule shop premises for his J S TRADERS for Commercial Purpose, The OWNER has agreed for the same.

The OWNER and the TENANT are making this Rental Agreement on the following terms and conditions:

  1. This RENTAL AGREEMENT is for a period of eleven months from the date of execution of this agreement i.e., on 25/02/2027.
  2. The OWNER has agreed to let out the said premises for a monthly rent of Rs.10,500.00 (Rupees Ten Thousand Five Hundred only)
  3. The TENANT has agreed to pay the rental amount on 15th of every month.
  4. The TENANT has agreed to pay 5% increase in the rent every 11 months, over the previous monthly rent.
  5. The TENANT has paid Security deposited to the OWNER an amount of Rs 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only) by way of cash as an advance deposit amount. The OWNER has agreed to refund the said amount without interest to the TENANT while vacating the rented premises. The TENANT has agreed for the same. The OWNER hereby acknowledges the receipt of the said amount from the tenant.
  6. The TENANT should use the rented premises for his Business Purpose Only, and not for any illegal trade or business and unlawful purpose like to endanger the building.
  7. The TENANT should pay the Electricity and water charges utilized for his own use as per the actual meter reading for the rented premises during the period of tenancy.
  8. The tenancy period may be renewed for further period of 11 months by mutual agreement between the OWNER and TENANT on the terms and conditions to be specified at that time.
  9. The OWNER and TENANT have agreed that three months prior notice on either side is required for the termination of the tenancy period.

IN WITNESSES THEREOF THE OWNER and the TENANT have signed this rental agreement on the day, month and year mentioned above.
